# Open Hype Product Context

## Register

Product. The interface serves browsing, playback, favorites, Android Auto, and offline listening. Design should feel refined and trustworthy, not decorative.

## Users

- Hype Machine listeners who want a native, reliable replacement for the legacy APK.
- Drivers using projected Android Auto or Android Automotive OS who need fast, glanceable browse and playback.
- Power listeners who favorite tracks, replay queues, and expect media controls to behave like Spotify or YouTube Music.

## Product Purpose

Open Hype is an unofficial native Android client for Hype Machine. It should make discovery and playback feel immediate on phone and safe in the car, while preserving Android media conventions.

## Design Principles

- Playback first: controls must be reachable, responsive, and predictable.
- Editorial warmth: orange, cream, and near-black surfaces should feel music-focused without copying Hype Machine or Android Auto templates.
- Glanceability: Android Auto surfaces should stay compact and system-native.
- Native behavior: media sessions, notifications, lock screen, autofill, focus, and accessibility matter as much as visuals.
- No fake affordances: visible controls must do something useful.

## Anti-References

- Generic purple-on-white AI app styling.
- Heavy glassmorphism, decorative gradients, or animations that slow repeated actions.
- Custom car UI that fights the system Media Templates.
- Oversized phone layouts that break on Samsung display scaling.
